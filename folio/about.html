[The Artist]

[Elena Rostova]

[Visual Artist & Editorial Photographer based between New York and Paris.]

[Born in Saint Petersburg and educated at the École des Beaux-Arts in Paris, my artistic foundation began in classical monochrome printmaking. This background informs my photographic sensibility: an obsession with textural grain, tonal restraint, and the tactile relationship between human bodies and raw architectural materials.]

[Over the past decade, my studio has produced campaigns and visual identities for independent fashion houses, ceramic sculptors, and architectural publications. My work seeks to strip away commercial hyperbole, creating space for stillness, natural sunlight, and unspoken tension.]

[When not on commercial commission, I travel extensively with medium format analog cameras documenting heritage architecture across Japan, Southern Italy, and the Nordic coastlines.]
