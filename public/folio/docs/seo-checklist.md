# SEO Checklist for Artists & Photographers · Folio

Follow this 10-minute checklist before announcing your website:

- [ ] **Title Tags**: Update `<title>` on every page to include your specific city or creative specialization (e.g. `Julian Vance · Editorial & Fashion Photographer in New York`). Keep titles under 60 characters.
- [ ] **Meta Descriptions**: Verify the `<meta name="description">` on each page summarizes your offerings in under 155 characters.
- [ ] **Canonical URL**: In each page's `<link rel="canonical">`, replace `https://julianvance.com/` with your actual live domain.
- [ ] **Social Sharing Cards**: Replace `assets/img/og-image.webp` (1200×630px) with your signature branded image for rich previews when shared on Pinterest, iMessage, Twitter, and LinkedIn.
- [ ] **Image Alt Text**: Replace placeholder `alt="..."` descriptions on each photograph with descriptive language that visually articulates the subject matter and lighting style.
- [ ] **Schema.org Structured Data**:
  - `index.html` & `about.html` include `@type: Person` schema. Update `name`, `jobTitle`, and `addressLocality`.
  - `portfolio.html` includes `@type: ImageGallery` schema.
- [ ] **Sitemap & Robots**:
  - Open `sitemap.xml` and replace `https://julianvance.com` with your production URL.
  - Check `robots.txt` to confirm all public URLs are open for search engine indexing.
- [ ] **Google Search Console**: Once your custom domain is live, submit your `sitemap.xml` URL in Google Search Console to expedite search indexing.
